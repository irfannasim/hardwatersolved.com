### Dealer brands (category 9)
- **Culligan: No.** Amazon's Culligan results are whole-house filter housings, cartridges, salt and parts. The one "softener" listing (B0C7HLCJHS) is a Fleck softener bundled with a Culligan filter.
- **Kinetico: No.** Only replacement filters, seal kits, the K12561 pre-filter string and repair books.
- **EcoWater: No, not under the EcoWater name.** Only parts and connector kits. EcoWater's parent company does sell on Amazon, but under other brand names. Morton MSD34C's Amazon slug reads "ECOWATER-SYSTEMS-8708", and ASIN B07D6XK5PV (slug "ECOWATER-SYSTEMS-LLC") resolves to "EcoPure EP42007 … Quantity 4".
- **RainSoft: No.** Only replacement RO filters and parts.
- **Hague: No.** No Hague whole-house units appeared in any result.
- Rheem and EcoWater have no smart/Wi-Fi softener on Amazon. GE GXSHC40N is the only confirmed Wi-Fi softener (category 3 has one product).
