Researched 2026-09-29. Every ASIN below was seen in an amazon.com result URL. Nothing is guessed.
**Price caveat:** amazon.com pages returned no price when fetched (they only gave the title, or failed with HTTP 500). Most prices therefore come from the brand's own store and are marked approx. An Amazon price may differ from the brand-store price, so re-check it before publishing.
