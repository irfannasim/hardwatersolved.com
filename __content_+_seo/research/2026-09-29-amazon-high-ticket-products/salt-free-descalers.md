## Salt-free TAC/NAC conditioners

### SpringWell FutureSoft Salt-Free Water Softener (FS1, 1-3 bathrooms)
- ASIN: B07NP8JY4G  | URL: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07NP8JY4G
- Price: $1,450–$1,725 approx (qualitywaterlab.com / waterfilterguru.com, citing SpringWell direct)
- Specs: TAC media. No salt, no backwash, no electricity. FS1 has a 12 GPM service flow (FS4: 15 GPM, 4-6 bathrooms). Rated up to 81 gpg hardness, best for 25 gpg or less. Iron must be under 0.3 ppm and manganese must be pretreated, otherwise the media fouls.
- Warranty / guarantee: Lifetime warranty on media, tanks, valves, bypass and heads. 6-month money-back guarantee, according to SpringWell and reviewers.
- Fits categories: 1
- Notes: Other SpringWell salt-free listings on Amazon: filter + salt-free combo B086Q59XHM; cartridge system with carbon + sediment + salt-free B0CH26B359 (price unverified); and "Salt-Free Water Softener Alternative by SpringWell" B0GYCL1C3V (new listing, price unverified).

### SpringWell Whole House Well Water Filter + Salt-Free Conditioner Combo (WSSF)
- ASIN: B0FT44VNJR  | URL: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FT44VNJR
- Price: from $3,599 approx (springwellwater.com / search snippet)
- Specs: AIO air-injection oxidizing filter plus FutureSoft TAC. The AIO removes up to 7 ppm iron, 1 ppm manganese and 8 ppm hydrogen sulfide before the TAC stage.
- Warranty / guarantee: Lifetime warranty and 6-month money-back guarantee (SpringWell standard terms).
- Fits categories: 1, 2
- Notes: This is the clearest example of "salt-free for well water with the pretreatment it needs."

### Pentair Pelican NS3-P NaturSoft Water Softener Alternative (1-3 bathrooms)
- ASIN: B000NG3YJU  | URL: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000NG3YJU
- Price: $1,587.99 approx (filtersfast.com)
- Specs: NAC (NaturSoft). Rated to 75 gpg hardness. Claims 99.6% effective under DVGW (listed as "DVGW-9191"; this is the listing's wording and is not verified as W512). Iron, manganese, sulfur and tannin must all be removed first. Includes a sediment prefilter (change every 6-9 months). Tank 1 media lasts 5 years or 600,000 gal; tank 2 media is lifetime. You still get some spotting, but it wipes off more easily.
- Warranty / guarantee: 12-year limited warranty on tanks, valves, heads, bypass and housings (Pelican warranty text via search).
- Fits categories: 1
- Notes: A dealer blog (midatlanticwater.net, "What Happened to the Brand") suggests Pelican's status has changed. Confirm the listing is still active and in stock before recommending.
