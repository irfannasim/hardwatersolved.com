A **Culligan water softener rental** usually costs about $50 a month, before salt, at the dealers whose prices are published, and introductory offers can start far lower. That monthly fee buys the softener, its installation and its service, with no large upfront payment. What it costs over time depends on how long you stay. At $50 a month, renting costs more than buying an online softener installed by a plumber in under three years, and more than a typical Culligan purchase in about five. Before you sign, the agreement terms matter as much as the rate.

## What Culligan Rental Costs per Month

Culligan is a franchise network. Each independently operated dealer sets its own rental rate, so there is no national price. These are the published figures we could find:

| Source | Monthly figure | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Culligan dealer location pages (e.g. Waukesha, WI; Mason City, IA; Leadington, MO), checked 2 October 2026 | **Starting at $50 a month** | "Get soft water service starting at $50/month with no large upfront payments. Offer expires 10/31." Marked "Limited time offer; offers may vary by dealer" |
| Introductory dealer offers | $9.95 a month, or $0, for the first three months | Run by some dealers, including Canadian locations; the regular rate applies afterwards |
| Upstate New York homeowner's quotes | $55 to $120 a month | The same dealers quoted $2,500 to $5,500 to buy |

Three things follow. The $50 figure is a **starting** price, so a larger tank, a twin-tank unit or added filtration will cost more. An introductory rate tells you nothing about month four, so ask for the regular rate in writing. And salt is not part of the headline figure at many dealers; delivery is a separate service.

You can see the published offer on [Culligan of Waukesha's location page](https://www.culliganwater.com/locations/culligan-of-waukesha). Your own dealer's page may show a different figure or none.

### A first-year rental bill, worked through

The monthly figure is only one line of the first-year bill. Here is an illustrative year for a family of four on 15 grains per gallon water, with a three-month introductory offer:

| Line | Assumption | First-year cost |
|---|---|---|
| Introductory months | 3 months at $9.95 | about $30 |
| Regular months | 9 months at $50 | $450 |
| Installation fee | Ask your dealer; some waive it | $0 to the dealer's figure |
| Salt | About 9 bags of 40 lb a year; $8 a bag if you buy your own | about $74, or the dealer's delivered price times 9 |
| **Total before installation and delivery charges** | | **about $554** |

*Salt use is from the household model in the site's Culligan research: about 4,500 grains a day at 15 gpg, 300 gallons a day, on an efficiency-rated 6 lb dose, which comes to roughly 330 to 370 lb a year.*

Year two has no introductory months, so it costs about $600 in rent plus salt. That is why the regular rate matters far more than the promotion: over five years, a $9.95 introductory quarter saves about $120 against $50 a month, while a regular rate of $60 instead of $50 adds $600.

## Culligan Water Softener Rental vs Buying: The Crossover Month

Renting avoids the purchase price; buying avoids the monthly fee. The crossover is the month at which the rent you have paid passes the cost of owning. The chart compares $50 a month in rent with two purchases:

- **An online softener installed by a plumber, about $1,400.** For example, an AFWFilters Fleck 5600SXT 48,000-grain softener plus roughly $600 of plumber labor and fittings. [Check price on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00YFOTWZG)
- **A Culligan purchase, an illustrative $2,500.** That is the bottom of the upstate New York purchase quotes above. Many Culligan purchases cost more.

Both purchase lines include **$7 a month** of owner upkeep, which is a $25 yearly allowance for supplies plus $60 a year set aside towards resin or valve repairs. Salt is left out on both sides, because a renter buys or orders salt too.

**What ten years costs.** At $50 a month, ten years of rent is $6,000. Owning the plumber-installed online softener for the same ten years costs about $2,240 with upkeep, and the illustrative $2,500 Culligan purchase about $3,340. If the rent rises 5% a year, ten years of rental comes to about $7,550, and the crossover against the $2,500 purchase moves forward to about month 53.

Two cases flip the answer. If you will move within about two years, renting is usually cheaper, provided the installation and removal fees are small. And if your dealer's rent is low while its purchase price is high, the crossover can sit well beyond the time you expect to stay.

## The 7 Agreement Terms to Check Before Signing

The rental agreement decides what the monthly figure buys. Get each of these in writing:

1. **The regular monthly rate.** Not the promotion. Ask what you will pay in month four and in year two.
2. **Rate increases.** How often the dealer can raise the rent, by how much, and how much notice you get. A rent that rises each year shortens every crossover in the table.
3. **Salt delivery.** Whether salt is included, the price per bag and per delivery, the delivery schedule, and whether you may buy your own. Over a year, delivered salt can rival the rent itself.
4. **Installation fee.** Some rentals charge one; others waive it. Ask what plumbing, drain and outlet work it covers, because a house without a softener loop needs the same site work whether the unit is rented or bought.
5. **Minimum term and early exit.** Whether there is a fixed first term, how much notice ends the agreement afterwards, and what you pay to leave early.
6. **Removal fee.** What it costs to take the unit out when you cancel or move, and whether the dealer restores the bypass or leaves capped pipes.
7. **Rent-to-own credit.** Whether any rent counts toward buying the unit, what share, and the buyout price after one, two and three years.

![Two people signing a printed agreement on a desk with a calculator beside it, the moment to confirm the regular rate, salt delivery and removal fee on a softener rental](https://images.pexels.com/photos/8293764/pexels-photo-8293764.jpeg)

If the rental is sold in your home, the FTC's [Cooling-Off Rule](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buyers-remorse-ftcs-cooling-rule-may-help) generally gives you until midnight of the third business day to cancel a sale of $25 or more made there. Use that window to compare the regular rate against the crossover table.

## What the Monthly Fee Usually Covers

A Culligan rental is a service product. The dealer owns the equipment, installs it and maintains it. That is the main advantage over buying.

| Usually covered by the rental | Ask about | Usually your cost |
|---|---|---|
| The softener and brine tank | Which model and tank size | Salt, unless delivery is included |
| Routine service and repairs on dealer-owned equipment | Response time; any trip or visit charge | Water and electricity the softener uses |
| Programming and adjustments | Whether a water test is included | Damage from freezing or misuse, if the agreement says so |
| Replacement if the unit fails | Whether you get the same model | Plumbing changes inside your house |

Compare that with ownership. Culligan's [high efficiency softener owner's guide](https://www.culligan.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01pdf/HE1_Water_Softener_Owners_Guide.pdf) gives a buyer lifetime coverage on the tank and resin and 10 years on the circuit board and valve body, but the warranty goes to the original end-user, and after the first year you pay freight and the local dealer's charges for repairs. A renter does not carry that labor risk. An owner does, which is what the $7 a month upkeep allowance in the model stands for.

## Renting a Culligan as a Tenant or a Landlord

A rental can be the right answer for people who cannot or should not buy:

- **Tenants.** A dealer-owned unit leaves with you. You still need the landlord's written consent before anything is connected to the plumbing, and an agreement on who pays for the loop and drain. The usual landlord-tenant split is set out in [water softeners in rental properties](/blog/water-softeners-in-rental-properties/).
- **Landlords.** Renting passes service to the dealer, which can suit a property you do not visit. Check whether the agreement can transfer between tenants or must be in the owner's name, and who is billed if a tenant stops paying.
- **Short stays.** If you expect to sell within two or three years, the crossover table shows renting is likely to cost less, as long as installation and removal fees are low.

## When a Culligan Rental Is the Wrong Choice

- **You will stay more than five years.** At $50 a month, even an illustrative $2,500 Culligan purchase costs less after about 59 months.
- **The rent is above about $80.** At that level, a plumber-installed online softener pays for itself in under two years.
- **Salt delivery is compulsory and priced high.** That turns a $50 rental into a much larger monthly bill.
- **The agreement allows open-ended rate rises** or charges a large removal fee. Both shift the risk onto you without the benefits of owning.

If you do rent, keep the agreement, note the regular rate and the date it starts, and diary the end of the minimum term. That is when you can renegotiate, buy out the unit or cancel.
