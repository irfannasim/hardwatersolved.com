## What a 32,000 Grain Softener Really Delivers

The "32,000" is a statement of resin volume. The industry convention puts about 32,000 grains in a cubic foot of standard resin, and that figure is the top of the capacity curve, reached only at a heavy salt dose of around 15 lb per cubic foot. Design tables published in *Water Conditioning & Purification* put a cubic foot of standard resin at about 21,000 grains at 6 lb of salt and about 31,000 at 15 lb. The full table, and why the box number overstates real capacity, is in [advertised vs usable softener capacity](/blog/advertised-vs-usable-softener-capacity/).

So a 32k unit programmed for efficiency delivers about **20,000 to 21,000 grains** per regeneration. This page uses 20,000, the conservative end. What the rating means on a spec sheet is covered in [what grain capacity means](/blog/what-does-grain-capacity-mean/).

That gap is why many small-home buyers are told a 32k will last 10 days and then watch it regenerate every five. Nothing is wrong with it. The estimate simply used the box number.

## Days Between Regenerations: When 32k Is Enough

Your daily hardness load is people × 75 gallons × hardness in gpg. Penn State Extension's guide to [water softening](https://extension.psu.edu/water-softening) uses the same 75 gallon per person figure in its worked example. Take one day's load off the 20,000 as a reserve, then divide what is left by the daily load:

![Grid of days between regenerations for a 32,000 grain water softener for one to four people at 10, 15 and 20 gpg, colour-coded from too small to too large](/diagrams/32000-grain-water-softener-days-between-regenerations-grid.svg "Two or three people on moderate water is the 32k sweet spot")

| Household | 10 gpg | 15 gpg | 20 gpg |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 person | 25.7 days | 16.8 days | 12.3 days |
| 2 people | 12.3 days | 7.9 days | 5.7 days |
| 3 people | 7.9 days | 4.9 days | 3.4 days |
| 4 people | 5.7 days | 3.4 days | 2.3 days |

How to read it:

- **3 to 8 days: well sized.** The bed cycles often enough to stay fresh and not so often that it wastes water on backwash and rinse.
- **8 to 14 days: a little large.** Fine, but set the valve's day override to 14 days and keep the salt dose low.
- **Over 14 days: too large.** One person on moderate water does not need a 32k. Resin sitting idle for weeks is the problem described in [can a water softener be too large](/blog/can-a-water-softener-be-too-large/). A 24k unit, or a 32k with a 7 to 14 day override, is the fix.
- **Under 3 days: false economy.** Four people on 20 gpg water would regenerate a 32k every two days or so. Each regeneration costs salt, water and wear, and the frequent cycles cancel out the lower purchase price. Buy a 48k.

On a well, add about 4 gpg to your hardness for every 1 ppm of dissolved iron before using the table. The method for the whole house is in [how to size a water softener](/blog/how-to-size-a-water-softener/).

## Which 32k Pick for Which Water

![Decision chart for choosing a 32,000 grain water softener by water source: city water by priority, well water by whether iron or manganese is present](/diagrams/32000-grain-water-softener-pick-by-water-source-chart.svg "Size first with the grid, then let your water choose the resin")

- **City water with chlorine or chloramine:** 10% crosslink resin, so the AFW Fleck 5600SXT or the SoftPro Elite. If chloramine is high, a carbon filter ahead of the softener protects any resin.
- **Clean well water with no chlorine:** standard resin is fine, so the DuraWater unit.
- **Well water with iron or manganese:** none of these standard-resin picks as they stand. Treat the iron first, or choose a fine-mesh resin system.
- **Tight budget on city water:** the Aquasure Harmony, programmed from 20,000 grains and registered for the full warranty.

## Setting Up a 32k So It Delivers

1. **Test your hardness** with a kit at the tap. Do not use the city's average.
2. **Program capacity from the usable number:** about 20,000 grains at 6 lb of salt per cubic foot, with the valve's reserve or one day of load held back.
3. **Set a day override** of 7 to 14 days if the grid puts you above 8 days.
4. **Keep the salt dose low.** Each pound above about 9 lb per cubic foot buys very little extra capacity.
5. **Install it ahead of the water heater**, with a bypass and an air gap on the drain line.

## The Bottom Line

A 32,000 grain softener is the right size for two or three people on moderate water, and the wrong size for one person on soft-ish water or four people on hard water. Size it from 20,000 usable grains, not 32,000. For most city homes, the best 32,000 grain water softener is the AFWFilters Fleck 5600SXT with 10% resin, the best balance of serviceability, resin and price. The Aquasure Harmony is the budget pick if you register it, and the SoftPro Elite is worth its premium only where low salt use matters for its own sake.
