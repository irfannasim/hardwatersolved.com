## How the Ten-Year Cost Was Worked Out

> **Methodology.** Every pick is a metered, salt-based softener listed on Amazon.com. Each one is costed for a family of four on 15 gpg chlorinated city water, using the same inputs as our [total cost of owning a water softener](/blog/total-cost-of-owning-a-water-softener/) model: 300 gallons a day, 3,000 grains removed per pound of salt, $7 a bag and $12 per 1,000 gallons of water and sewer. Installation is an illustrative $400 for a plumber fitting the unit into an existing loop. Parts were costed from the brand's own parts store. Nothing was physically tested. **Excluded:** timer-only units, salt-free conditioners, and units shown as unavailable on Amazon.

## 1. Aquasure Harmony 32k: Best Budget Softener Overall

Also sold at Home Depot and Aquasure's own store.

[Check price on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07F18VG8L)

The Harmony 32k is a conventional two-tank softener: a 1 cu ft resin tank, a separate brine tank, and Aquasure's Aquatrol digital metered valve. Aquasure sizes it for one or two bathrooms.

**Why it ranks first.** Aquasure lists every major part on its own store, with a price. On 1 October 2026 that included a seal and spacer assembly, the circuit board with display, a complete replacement control valve and a pre-filled 1 cu ft resin tank. That makes it possible to cost the whole decade, which is the test this page sets.

**Warranty.** The current [Harmony owner's manual](https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/d3/d38453b1-ce56-4e0a-859c-27e296a759d7.pdf) gives two years without registration and five years on the valve, electronics and resin if you register. The resin tank and brine tank are warranted for ten years. Read the exclusion: resin and internal valve parts are not covered on water with iron, manganese or very high chlorine.

**Usable capacity.** One cubic foot of resin gives about 21,000 grains at an efficient 6 lb of salt and about 26,000 at 9 lb, not 32,000. For a family of four on 15 gpg, using 4,500 grains a day, that is a regeneration every three and a half to five days after a day of reserve. That is fine, but a household larger than four should move up a size. The full method is in [advertised vs usable softener capacity](/blog/advertised-vs-usable-softener-capacity/).

**Skip it if** you want parts you can buy from any supplier. The Aquatrol valve is Aquasure's own, so parts come from Aquasure.

Also sold at AFWFilters.

[Check price on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B071JCX9NK)

This is the unit the budget picks have to beat. It is the same 1 cu ft of resin as the Harmony 32k, so its capacity at any salt setting is the same. The differences are in what lasts and what is repairable.

- **The resin is 10% crosslink,** which resists chlorine better than standard resin. On city water it is more likely to last ten years. It costs more than the Harmony 32k up front. Over ten years the gap shrinks, and almost disappears if the resin outlasts the decade. That is the honest verdict: a mid-priced Fleck is not cheaper to own than a well-supported budget unit, but it is the safer bet if you plan to keep the softener for fifteen years.

## Where a Cheap Softener Really Costs More

The running cost hardly varies between metered units, so the extra cost of a cheap softener shows up in four places.

1. **Parts you cannot buy.** A valve whose maker stops selling the board, or never published a parts list, turns a circuit-board repair into a new softener. GE's cabinet softeners are now listed as no longer manufactured. Owners will depend on whatever stock third-party sellers have left.
2. **A timer instead of a meter.** A time-clock valve regenerates on schedule whether the resin needs it or not. That can add hundreds of dollars of salt and water over ten years, more than the saving on the box. None of the picks here is a timer unit.
3. **Undersizing.** A bed too small for the house regenerates every day or two. Salt per grain stays similar, but the valve cycles far more often and wears faster. Size from usable grains with [how to size a water softener](/blog/how-to-size-a-water-softener/).
4. **Paid maintenance.** A $150 annual service visit costs $1,500 over ten years, likely more than the softener itself. All four picks are simple enough to look after yourself. A typical owner's maintenance budget is set out in [annual water softener maintenance cost](/blog/annual-water-softener-maintenance-cost/).

## What Budget Softeners Give Up

Spending less does not mean softer or harder water. Ion exchange works the same in a budget unit as in a dealer system. What you give up is:

- **Capacity per dollar on big households.** Budget units top out around 32,000 grains. Large homes on hard water outgrow them.
- **Iron tolerance.** None of these four is sold as an iron filter. Water with significant iron or manganese needs fine-mesh resin or pretreatment.
- **Warranty conditions.** Most five-year cover depends on registration within a set number of days. Some depends on buying the brand's own cleaner. Register on the day you install.
- **Certification.** Check whether a unit is certified to NSF/ANSI 44 before you rely on its efficiency claims. The EPA's WaterSense notes on [cation exchange water softeners](https://www.epa.gov/watersense/cation-exchange-water-softeners) explain why demand-initiated regeneration and rated salt efficiency matter for water use.

## Who Should Buy None of These

- **Households of five or more on hard water.** Buy a larger bed. A budget 32k will regenerate every two or three days and still run short on heavy days.
- **Well owners with iron, manganese or sulfur.** Fix those first. A cheap softener fed iron fouls quickly and voids the resin warranty.
- **Anyone renting.** A plumbed-in softener is a poor fit for a rental. A portable or exchange-tank service may cost less overall.
- **Anyone not willing to register the unit.** The five-year cover on every Aquasure pick depends on registration. Without it, the ten-year cost above is optimistic.

Choose the best budget water softener on its ten-year cost, not its price tag. A well-supported budget unit and a mid-priced Fleck end up close together over ten years. An unsupported cheap unit can end up costing the price of a second softener.
