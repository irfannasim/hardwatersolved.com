**Home Depot vs Lowe's water softeners is mostly a question of brands, not stores, because the two shelves barely overlap.** On 2 October 2026 Home Depot's listings were built around Rheem, GE, WaterBoss, Aquasure and APEC, from about $489 to $750. Lowe's was built around Whirlpool and A.O. Smith, from about $479 to $719. The prices overlap almost completely. The most useful thing to know is that some models are twins under different badges. Home Depot's Rheem RHS32 at $489 and Lowe's Whirlpool WHES33 at $519 publish the same capacity figures and come from the same maker. Both stores install through independent contractors, so the store's name is not the installer's.

Three patterns stand out:

1. **Each store has its own cabinet brands.** Rheem and GE are Home Depot names in this snapshot; Whirlpool and A.O. Smith are Lowe's names. A.O. Smith runs a dedicated site for its Lowe's range, [aosmithatlowes.com](https://www.aosmithatlowes.com/products/water-softeners/ao-wh-soft-350c), which lists the 350C, 400T and 450T with Lowe's item numbers.
2. **Home Depot carries more two-tank systems.** The Aquasure Harmony units are conventional resin-tank-plus-brine-tank softeners. The Lowe's snapshot has no two-tank system that we could identify.
3. **The model number is capacity at the heaviest salt dose.** A "44" or "45" will not deliver that at a normal salt setting. ## Twin Models Across the Aisle

Several big-box softener brands are badges on one maker's platform. NSF's certification listings name **Water Channel Partners** of Eagan, Minnesota behind Whirlpool, Rheem, Morton and EcoPure softeners. That lets you compare across the two stores:

| Home Depot | Lowe's | What is the same | What differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rheem RHS32, $489 | Whirlpool WHES33, $519 | Capacity at all three salt doses, 0.88 cu ft of resin, 105 gpg limit, same maker | Rated 4,940 gr/lb (Rheem manual) vs 4,950 (Whirlpool product page); iron rating 8 vs 9 ppm; $30 |

The rest of the two lineups are not twins. The Rheem RHS42 (1.2 cubic feet of resin, 9.9 gpm) and the Whirlpool WHES44 (1.23 cubic feet, 8.2 gpm on NSF's listing) are close in size, but their flow ratings differ. A.O. Smith and GE come from different makers altogether: A.O. Smith warrants its own Lowe's range, and GE Appliances, a Haier company, warrants GE's.

## Installation: What Each Store's Service Promises

Neither store sends its own employees to plumb in a softener. Both use independent installers, and the terms differ.

**Lowe's.** Its water softener installation page, as indexed on 2 October 2026, says:

- "All installers are background checked, insured and licensed (where applicable)."
- Installation "includes materials, disposal, testing and initial supply of salt if applicable."
- It does not include structural modifications or commercial installations.
- The installation is guaranteed for one year, with Lowe's warranting that the work will be performed by an independent installer "in a good and workmanlike manner".

**Home Depot.** Its water treatment service pages, as indexed the same day, describe:

- Installers who are "local, licensed, insured and have undergone a thorough background-screening process".
- A free in-home consultation that includes a water test and a basic plumbing inspection, with a system recommended afterwards.
- Pricing set after that visit, rather than a fixed install fee on the product page.

Third-party write-ups describe a one-year labor guarantee on Home Depot installations too. We could not open the terms page to confirm it, so get the labor guarantee written into your contract.

The practical difference: Lowe's describes an install of a unit you choose. Home Depot's in-home route starts with a consultation and ends with a quote. In both cases ask for the line items. A loop, a drain run with an air gap, an outlet and a permit can easily double the job, and they are broken down in [water softener installation cost](/blog/water-softener-installation-cost/). Fixr's national [installation cost guide](https://www.fixr.com/costs/water-softener-installation) puts labor alone at about $150 to $1,000.

If you would rather fit it yourself, the job is manageable in a house with an existing loop. The steps are in [how to install a whole-house water softener](/blog/how-to-install-a-whole-house-water-softener/).

- **GE's older GXSH40V** is discontinued; its 2025 replacement, the GXSH35W, is on Amazon at about $699.
- **Aquasure Harmony 48,000** was $629.99 at both Home Depot and Aquasure's own store, and its Amazon listing is live.

So for now the stores are where most of these units can actually be bought. Where an Amazon listing does exist, compare the delivered price and whose warranty and returns terms apply.

## Five Checks Before You Buy at Either Store

1. **Read the box for the salt dose behind the grain number.** If the box or manual gives capacity at several doses, size from the middle one.
2. **Check the flow rating and pressure drop.** A cabinet rated at 7 to 8 gpm suits two bathrooms, not four.
3. **Find the iron rating and the type of iron it covers.** Every cabinet here is rated for clear-water (ferrous) iron only.
4. **Note the warranty conditions.** Registration deadlines and cleanser purchases decide whether the long number on the box applies.
5. **Ask about the softener salt.** Both stores sell it in 40 lb bags; Lowe's listed Morton 40 lb pellets at $7.38 to $9.38 on 2 October 2026. A year's salt for a family of four on 15 gpg can run to ten bags or more, so budget for it from the start.

## When to Skip Both Stores

- **Well water with more than a few ppm of iron, manganese or sulfur.** The cabinet units are rated for clear-water iron only, and none of them is a filter. Test first and plan any pre-treatment before you buy.
- **Peak flow above about 8 gpm.** Most cabinets in both lineups are rated near 8 gpm. A large house with several bathrooms in use at once needs a bigger valve and resin bed than either shelf offers.
- **Very hard water in a big household.** A 1-cubic-foot cabinet regenerates every two to three days at 25 gpg for five people. A larger two-tank system bought online, or a dealer system, will suit better.
- **Buyers who want no salt.** Both stores also sell salt-free units, but those condition water rather than soften it. The trade-offs are in [salt-based vs salt-free water treatment](/blog/salt-based-vs-salt-free-water-treatment/).

The short version of Home Depot vs Lowe's water softeners: Home Depot has more brands and the two-tank Aquasure units; Lowe's has Whirlpool and the longest unconditional cabinet warranty in A.O. Smith. For a mid-size cabinet, check both, because the Rheem and Whirlpool twins cost different amounts for the same design.
